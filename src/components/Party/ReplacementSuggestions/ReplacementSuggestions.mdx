# ReplacementSuggestions

The Party Analyzer's stretch goal: suggests swapping the party's weakest offensive contributor for up to three Pokémon from the same generation that would leave the party covering more types. The ranking is `rankReplacements` (`@utils/partyAnalyzer`).

## Behaviour

- `party` is the six slots (empty ones `null`), so the suggestion can name the member's real slot.
- The weakest contributor is the member adding the fewest types nobody else hits for 2×; a tie goes to the highest dex number.
- Candidates are default forms (no Mega or Gigantamax) of the same `generationId`, from species not already in the party.
- They are ranked by net coverage gain: how many more types the party would cover with the candidate in that slot. That is the gaps it closes minus the types only the current member covered, so a swap that trades one gap for another is never suggested. Ties go to the lower dex number, and at most three are shown as cards (sprite, number, name, type pills).
- The whole section is hidden with fewer than two members, with full coverage, or when no candidate has a net gain.
