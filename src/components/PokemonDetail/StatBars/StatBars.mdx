# StatBars

The six base stats as bars, with the total underneath.

## Behaviour

- Stats are in the order HP, Attack, Defense, Sp. Atk, Sp. Def, Speed; each bar is an accessible `meter`.
- Bar length is the stat's share of the highest possible stat (`MAX_STAT` in `@utils/stats`) and is capped at full width.
